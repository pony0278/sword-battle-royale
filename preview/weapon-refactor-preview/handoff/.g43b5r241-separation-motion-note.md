- Coupling remains authoritative until release.
- Normal Parry enters an explicit `separation` phase for ~78 ms and targets ~6.5 cm weapon/right-arm displacement from the captured coupling-release pose.
- Perfect Parry targets ~9.5 cm over ~86 ms.
- Block does not enter the new separation phase.
- After separation, the existing B3 force chain continues through shoulder, torso/hips, and legs.
- The lab records actual sword-tip displacement from the coupling-release position so target motion and observed motion can be compared directly.
